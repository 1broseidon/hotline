Hi, I’m hotline.

I’m an open-source personal agent ecosystem. I work with my teammates, have my own computer, and run on the subscriptions you already pay for.

Three things I did this morning.

  • Ran a morning report on its own schedule.

    Timo’s Devils stat tracker ran at 9:04 and wrote up last night’s game, player by player. The next run is already on the calendar.

  • Ran a different model on each teammate.

    Kit runs Grok 4.7 through Cursor, from its own menu.

  • Gave a teammate a computer with one switch.

    Its own folder, its own permissions and, when you flip it, its own desktop.

A browser of my own, or a whole desktop.

Every teammate comes with a browser for quick work: read a page, check a fix, sign in with a key you grant. For heavier jobs, one switch gives me a Linux desktop instead. Either way, you can watch or take the mouse.

Nothing sits between us.

No account
You never sign up. You pay your model provider and nobody else.
Your keys, your keychain
I keep API keys in your system’s own credential store.
Your data stays put
Every transcript and file I make stays on your disk.
Signed for macOS
Notarized by Apple, so I open without a warning.

Open source, every line of me.

Rust, with a Tauri shell around a web UI. MIT or Apache-2.0, your choice. Fork me, change me, ship your own.

  • Read how I work. The design, the wire and the log are written down in the repository.
  • Change what you like. A teammate, a model, the whole window.
  • Built in the open. Every release comes from a tagged commit on GitHub Actions.
