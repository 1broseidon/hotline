## Hotline 0.49.0: what a teammate is on, what came of it, and a lighter window

- **What came of it, under the reply.** The line under a reply is now what the turn left behind, in the teammate's own words: what it sent, made or changed, what is still going, and what is left for you, a failure first and in red. Then the sites it read and how long it took. One press opens the whole path in the right-hand pane, every step under what the teammate said it was on.
- **What it is on, beside the mark.** While a teammate works, its mark in the message box moves by the kind of work it says it is on (thinking, reading, searching, making, or talking, which puts on a headset) with the teammate's own few words beside it. The desk no longer puts its own word for the work or a step count there.
- **An opening line that fits.** Teammates are asked to open with what a person would say to that request instead of a stock "On it", to skip it when the answer needs no work, and to draft and ask before sending something in your name, paying, cancelling or sharing your information.
- **Helpers, out of the conversation.** Subagents are called helpers. A helper or a command no longer gets a row of its own in the conversation: while it runs it is counted in the message box, and afterwards it is in the turn's path, where helpers started together are one line ("Started 4 helpers"). One that failed is marked on the line under the reply.
- **What is running, at a glance.** The message box says how many of a teammate's threads are running now ("2 threads ›"), two teammates talking included, and opens a short list of them whose last row is the full Threads list. The header's side-thread chips and the chips for each subagent are gone.
- **Sources are the pages it opened.** The pages a turn opened, by a fetch, a command, a browser or Claude Code's own web search, are its sources: a stack of site icons on the line under the reply, listed first in the path. Results a search only listed, and local pages such as localhost or a `.local` name, are not.
- **One Threads list.** Every work thread, handoff, helper, command, pair, call and job is one row in the right-hand pane, opening on what needs you, what is live, what is new and the last 24 hours. A Kind menu narrows it.
- **Watch the work.** A helper, handoff, pair, call, job or command opens in the right pane and shows its work as it happens, with Stop where it can be stopped.
- **Lighter and smoother.** A long conversation keeps only what is near the view in the page. With a 2,000-line thread loaded the window uses about half the memory it did, and scrolling, switching teammates and replies landing are smooth on Mac, Windows and Linux. Diagrams draw ahead of time, so scrolling past one no longer stalls.
- **Update tracks.** Settings › Updates has a Track: Stable for final releases, or Beta for the betas before them.
- **Fixed:** a long reply no longer stops the conversation updating as it ends; a permission card nobody answers says Expired instead of waiting forever; the plan usage card follows Claude's current limits and drops a limit the account no longer has, and the weekly line keeps its figure after a reply instead of showing a dash; with two or more desks in one window, an idle window no longer asks its desk every 4 seconds, which made its memory grow; the computer button is refreshed less often when the window is not in front; MCP sign-in no longer reaches into your network for a server on the internet, while servers on your own network or tailnet still sign in; updated SQLite and rmcp for four security advisories.

### Known limits

- On the Mac, the window's web process still grows slowly with use, much less than in 0.48. Restarting Hotline frees it.
- A page a teammate opened for a chore along the way, not for your answer, also shows as a source.
- Conversations from before this release keep their "Subagent" titles, and their paths can show a helper's start twice.
- The phone app's current build still says "Subagent", shows a row for each helper and does not show the new line under a reply. A phone update follows.
- A helper that fails to start, a command sent to the background, and Codex's use of the new `activity` and `outcome` tools were tested against fixtures, not in live runs.
