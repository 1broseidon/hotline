---
title: Notifications
description: What pings you, and when the phone stays quiet.
---

With notifications on, the phone hears:

- **Each finished reply**: one notification per turn, however long the turn
  was, showing the teammate's last message.
- **Every card that waits on you**, the moment it is asked, with the buttons
  to answer it (see [Talk to your teammates](/docs/phone/chat/)).

The phone stays quiet:

- **while you're at the desk**: if the Mac's Hotline window is in front and
  you've used it lately, nothing is sent to the phone;
- **for the conversation you have open**: the phone doesn't banner the
  conversation already on its screen.

**Settings → Notifications** on the phone opens the iPhone's settings for
Hotline.
