## Hotline 0.36.0: call your teammate, not a stand-in

- **One teammate, on the phone and in the chat.** A call goes straight into your teammate's own conversation, so it remembers the call as it remembers the chat, and nothing is lost in a handoff.
- **Heard short, written in full.** On a call your teammate answers twice: a quick spoken answer, and the complete written one in the chat, with code, tables and links. The chat shows what was said as a quiet line above the reply.
- **Cut in without tapping.** On the desktop, the call keeps listening while your teammate works. Just speak, and your words go into what it's already doing.
- **Better local transcription.** The desk's models listen for your teammates' names and words you add. Whisper and Moonshine join Parakeet, and the list stays tidy.
- **Subagents on Claude Code** no longer leave a teammate stuck on Working once it has answered.
