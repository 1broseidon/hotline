## Hotline 0.46.0: teammates that know their threads, and a desk that loses nothing

- **Aware of its threads.** Every turn starts with a short index of the teammate's other threads, what is at work, what waits on you and what ended today, sent whole once and then only as what moved. Ask "what's still out?" and it knows without reading anything.
- **Asides, not reports.** Threads tell each other things with quiet notes and nudges heard at the next turn, never in your chat. Talk in a thread a teammate handed over, Loop in… a colleague for one answer, or End thread. Built on Kelviny Moura's work in #203.
- **Steer the work in flight.** Type into a work thread mid-turn and the line joins that turn: at step three of five, "make it four" changes the rest. A colleague's follow-up does the same, and the handoff still comes back as one result.
- **Harness updates.** Settings → Updates shows one row per agent harness, says when Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another has an update, and installs it in the app.
- **Background work you can see.** A Claude Code teammate's background commands and subagents show at the composer's corner while they run, even after its turn ends.
- **Nothing lost, nothing stuck.** A restart keeps every waiting line, in threads too; Remote stays on after a bad boot; a phone's retried send gets a true answer; cards, sends, sign-in and "is it working" each hold one state, so nothing sticks or disappears.
- **Fixes.** Claude Code's model list keeps every model, an agent slow to reload its conversation no longer goes quiet, jobs keep their reports, calls recover from a false start and survive a hold, and teammate computers are made once and never idled under work.
