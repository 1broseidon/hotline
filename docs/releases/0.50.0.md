## Hotline 0.50.0: teammates message each other through an inbox

- **One way for teammates to reach each other.** A teammate sends a colleague a message, and it lands in that colleague's inbox. The colleague sees every new message together and decides what to do first, what to combine and what to leave for later, instead of being interrupted by each one. An idle teammate is woken a few seconds after a message lands; a busy one is told in one line and carries on.
- **You can tell what a colleague's message prompted.** A file, a card, a sign-in or credential request, or a schedule a teammate makes while working on one colleague's message says so in one quiet line, "From Goldie's message · title", which opens the message. A card that appears because another teammate asked for it never looks like your own request.
- **Messages handled quietly stay out of your chat.** When a teammate reads and sorts its inbox on its own, the chat shows one quiet line, "Inbox · read 3, sent 1", that opens to what happened. You hear from it only when something is yours to know: a result you asked for, a decision, or a problem.
- **See every message in a teammate's inbox.** Inbox in a teammate's menu, or "2 unread" in the message box, opens its messages: Open, Closed and Sent. Each says where it stands (Sent, Seen, Picked up, Later, Answered, Done, Expired) and opens to its words and every step it went through. You read; the teammates act.
- **A message picked up as work links to its work thread.** A message a teammate picked up has a row under it for its work thread, and the work thread's Message opens the message again. Back goes where you came from.
- **A message nobody picks up expires.** One left unopened or set aside for three days closes by itself, and its sender is told. If it came from something you asked for, the sender tells you in one line.
- **You decide when one teammate asks another with more access.** If a teammate asks a colleague that can reach your whole Mac, a card asks you first: Allow, Always or Deny. Between teammates with the same access nothing asks.
- **A new outside source is approved at your desk.** When a teammate proposes a source from outside Hotline, such as a webhook, your phone shows the card but cannot answer it: say yes or no at the desk.
- **Two teammates that keep messaging each other pause.** After twelve messages back and forth without you, they stop and ask you to Keep going or Stop.
- **A teammate working on its own pauses too.** After 20 quiet turns in an hour without you, it shows the same card. Change the number in Settings › General: "Pause a teammate after".
- **A job that asks a colleague gets the answer.** A reply to a message a scheduled job sent comes back into that job while its run is still going, so "ask Huck, then summarise" finishes. A one-time job, or a loop's last run, waits for the reply before it ends, and its row says "Waiting on Huck"; it waits at most three days, and Stop or End job ends it at once. Once a loop's next run starts, or the job ends, a late reply goes to the teammate's main chat quietly.
- **Closed messages are kept 30 days.** After that they are removed. Change it in Settings › General: "Keep closed messages for". Hotline also tidies the inbox's files each time it starts.
- **Conversations from before.** Teammates' conversations with each other from 0.49 are kept under Earlier in the Threads list, read-only, as "Conversation with Goldie". No new ones start.
- **Updating from 0.49.** A question one teammate had asked another and that was still open is ended, and the asker is told so by Hotline. A hand-off that was running finishes and reports to its teammate's chat as it did before, and a follow-up hand-off keeps its thread. A teammate that tries the old message tool is told to use the new one.

### Known limits

- Messages between teammates cannot yet cross to a teammate on a linked desk. 0.49 could pass a message to another desk, and 0.50 does not: a teammate that tries is told so.
- A loop's run that is not its last does not wait for a reply: one that comes after the loop's next run has started goes to the teammate's main chat, quietly.
