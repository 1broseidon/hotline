## Hotline 0.47.0: your colours, and tables you can read

- **An accent of your own.** Settings → Appearance → Accent picks one of six colours (Mint, Teal, Sky, Iris, Orchid, Rose) for your bubble, keys and selections. The room keeps it, so every window on the desk draws it; light or dark stays each window's own. Mint is still the default.
- **Teammates in their own colours.** A teammate answers in a soft tint of its face's hue, or grey when its face is gold. Its settings have a Bubble row to pick Grey or another of the six tints, and only that teammate's replies change.
- **Tables you can read.** A table in a reply keeps readable columns instead of breaking words a letter to a line. A bubble with a table can take the whole column, and a table wider than that scrolls across.
- **Light mode reads on white.** The small grey hints meet WCAG AA, fields, setting groups and the composer show their edges again, and the mint is a little deeper. Dark is unchanged.
