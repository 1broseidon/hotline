## Hotline 0.44.0: see the work, hear the calls, a calmer window

- **Watch the work, not the commands.** A teammate at work shows a card tucked behind the composer, with what it is doing in a few words and Stop. Tap it for a peek of the turn that slides up over the conversation; a line opens that step in the right-hand pane. Steps read as "Ran cargo test", "Read 3 files", "Used Linear twice", in the agent's own words when it gives them; the pane keeps every command as it ran.
- **Calls: Live or Turn by turn.** Pick a realtime model your providers run, or let the teammate answer on its own model while the desk transcribes and speaks. Each choice says what it costs and where it runs, and replies are shorter.
- **Ollama speaks.** Pull a speech model such as `phonon` into Ollama and pick its voice: with the desk's own hearing, a call needs no cloud provider at all.
- **Pictures and documents in the conversation.** What you send shows as itself, as on the phone.
- **A calmer, tidier window.** A roomier faces-only rail with the sidebar key in one place, a face that grows out to show its latest line, the model and effort always named, a call's card clear of the window's controls, and a teammate menu that opens on who it is.
- **Fixes.** The model picker lists only chat models, a long Turn by turn reply is said once, a yes/no answer reaches the teammate as yes or no, and the traffic lights sit right from the first launch.
