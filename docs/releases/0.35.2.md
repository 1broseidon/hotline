## Hotline 0.35.2: budgets you can read, and calls that sound like your teammate

- **Budgets.** Chat, Voice and Images each have their own daily and monthly limit, with no limit unless you set one. Settings › Providers shows what each one went on. Subscriptions and models on your computer are never counted.
- **Calls cost what they cost.** The call assistant was being charged far more than the provider billed, which could cut a call short. Every request is now settled to what it really cost.
- **More natural calls.** The call assistant answers in one reply, in your teammate's own voice, and hands anything that needs doing straight to the teammate.
- **Subagents don't hold up the chat.** When a teammate has answered and only its subagents are still working, the answer lands in the chat and a chip above the message box shows what's still running.
- **A stuck agent no longer stalls the desk.** An agent that never finishes starting now fails with an error that says why, and your other teammates keep working.
