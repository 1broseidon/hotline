## Hotline 0.42.0: call your teammate itself

- **The voice is your teammate.** On a live voice (Grok Voice, GPT Realtime, GPT-Live) you talk to the teammate itself: its name and manner, real work done on its own model in the first person. Talk over it and it stops and answers you; pause mid-sentence while dictating and it waits.
- **A call is a thread.** Every call keeps its transcript and tool calls. Hang up and it's parked: carry on by text, call it again, or say "like we said on the call" in your chat. One closing note follows, and calls close together share one.
- **Calling the desk reaches your team lead**, who answers who's on the team and hands named work straight to that teammate.
- **Cards on a call.** A card raised on a call lands in your chat; answer yes/no questions and approvals by voice. Credentials, 2FA, screens, payments and deletions wait for you at the desk.
- **Files and your name.** Files a call's work writes reach your chat, one card when there are many. Tell a teammate your name and it saves it once you approve.
- **Live means live.** A live voice never silently drops to Legacy, and a short drop holds the call for twenty seconds. Legacy voice keeps you posted while it works.
- **ChatGPT and Copilot sign-ins use the vault.** Existing sign-ins move into your operating system's credential store when first used, without signing you out. If the keychain is locked, Hotline asks you to unlock it and keeps the old credential until it can finish the move.
- **Downgrading requires sign-in.** Once a sign-in has moved, an older Hotline can't read it and shows sign-in required. Sign in again if you downgrade.
