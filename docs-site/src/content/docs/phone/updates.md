---
title: Updates
description: How the phone app keeps itself current.
---

Most updates arrive without a new install. The app looks for one each time
it comes to the front, and when a desk connects. When one has downloaded:

- The team list shows **Update ready · Restart**. Tap it to use the update now.
- Otherwise the app loads it the next time it goes to the background, so the
  new version is simply there when you come back.

**Settings → App** shows the version, when it last checked, and
**Check for updates**.

Some updates need a new build of the app. Those come through TestFlight
during the beta.
