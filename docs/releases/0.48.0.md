## Hotline 0.48.0: a browser for every teammate, and replies that read like a messenger

- **A browser of its own.** A teammate with no computer gets its own browser on the desk (macOS 14 and later, Linux and Windows). It reads pages, clicks, types and takes screenshots in a hidden window; a picture of the page sits in the corner of the conversation, and a click opens it to watch or take control. A local address opens only once you allow it, the desk's own ports never do, and a page never gets your mic or camera.
- **Sign-ins on ask.** The desk no longer offers your cookies every time a page loads. A teammate that needs to be signed in asks on one card: Use Cookies, Sign In or Open Browser. Add Cookies in its Secrets shares a site ahead of time, and Settings › Secrets › Cookie Sources says which of your browsers Hotline may ever read.
- **Phone width and more.** `viewport` shows a page as a phone, a tablet or a wide screen would, and the corner picture takes that shape.
- **Tables, code and diagrams that fit.** What a reply carries sits on one sheet in its bubble. Too big for it, it's cut with a line saying what it is, and a click opens it in the conversation's place, grown from the bubble, with Copy and ← back. One toolbar per bubble.
- **A tidier teammate pane.** Computer is the machine, Secrets is what it signs in with, Permissions is what it may reach, now with Local addresses.
- **Your own search.** SearXNG joins web search, off until you set it up.
- **ARM Linux.** The desktop app ships for aarch64 Linux too.
- **Fixed:** scrolling up a long conversation no longer jumps or sticks.
