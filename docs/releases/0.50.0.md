## Hotline 0.50.0

### Teammate inbox

- **Inbox routing.** Teammates message each other through an inbox instead of interrupting one another. Idle teammates wake shortly after a message arrives; busy ones get a one-line notice and keep working.
- **Inbox view.** Open a teammate's messages from Inbox in their menu: Open, Closed, and Sent, with status (Sent, Seen, Picked up, Later, Answered, Done, Expired) and full history. Picked-up messages link to their work thread. Nothing counts a teammate's unread messages: what it hasn't read is its work, and shows up as threads and cards, not mail.
- **Quieter chat.** Quiet inbox handling puts nothing in the chat; when the teammate writes to you, what it read and sent is on the line under its reply ("Sent a message"), and its path lists each message under Teammates. Asking a teammate to reach a colleague drops the separate "Asked Timo" row; the message row shows status and the reply lands below it. Cards and schedules created from a colleague's message (approvals, sign-in or credential requests, schedules) carry a clickable "From Goldie's message · title" line; replies and files don't.
- **Finished reports.** Chat stays quiet while teammates work on something you asked for. When the exchange completes, your teammate tells you what came of it.
- **Inbox work stays quiet.** A teammate handling its inbox doesn't show as working or blink in the team list. It shows as working again once you write to it.
- **Work for a colleague goes back to that colleague.** What a teammate does for a colleague's message, its result, files and follow-ups, goes back to the colleague, not into your chat.
- **Failed wakes are told.** When a teammate can't be woken for a message (out of quota, not signed in, stopped, or the agent won't start), the message stays unread and the sender gets a notice from Hotline. If the message was sent for you, your chat gets one line, with the time the quota resets when it's known.
- **Hand-offs open in one press.** Pressing a thread a teammate handed over, from Threads, the message box or the chat, opens the thread itself: the brief at the top from whoever handed it over, then the work as it happens. Who handed it over is said once, at the top.
- **Access approvals.** Messaging a colleague with whole-Mac access or on another agent like Codex ("Huck runs on Codex, which sets its own access.") holds the send behind Allow, Always or Deny; same-access teammates do not prompt. The send waits for your answer, so a teammate reports a message as sent only after you allow it. Proposed outside sources (such as webhooks) must be approved at the desk and show read-only on phone.
- **Activity pauses.** Two teammates pause after 12 exchanges without you, and one teammate pauses after 20 colleague-driven turns in an hour, asking Keep going or Stop. Change the hourly limit in Settings › General ("Pause a teammate that works 20 times an hour without you").
- **Scheduled job replies.** Jobs receive colleague replies while running. One-time jobs and a loop's last run wait up to three days ("Waiting on Huck", with Stop or End job to cancel); late replies go quietly to main chat.
- **Expiry and retention.** Unopened or deferred messages expire after three days and notify the sender. Closed messages are removed after 30 days; change this in Settings › General ("Keep closed messages for").
- **Upgrading from 0.49.** Earlier teammate conversations stay read-only under Earlier in Threads ("Conversation with Goldie"). Open 0.49 questions close and notify the sender; running handoffs finish as before.

### Calls and voice

- **Faster call pickup.** Turn-by-turn calls load the hearing model and wake the teammate's session as soon as you call. "Acknowledge while the teammate thinks" in Settings › Voice says a short spoken acknowledgment and progress line while the teammate works without interruption.
- **Interrupt by speaking.** On by default with Apple speech recognition: talk over a turn-by-turn reply to interrupt it. Best with headphones; switch it off in Settings › Voice.
- **The teammate knows what you heard.** After you cut in, the teammate is told how much of its reply you heard before it answers.
- **Built-in voice.** Speak locally with Gradium's Phonon 31M (English) under Settings › Voice › Speech. It downloads once (about 66 MB), needs no account or network, and starts speaking in a fraction of a second. Not on ARM Linux yet.
- **Voice settings.** Call settings and local hearing and speaking models (Voice models) move to Settings › Voice. Providers holds accounts, defaults, and budgets.

### Secrets

Improved security between the desk and a teammate's computer (requires computer 0.13; older computers keep working as before). This is hardening, not a guarantee: the app labels secrets "Readable by this teammate's programs" wherever that applies, and further hardening follows in later releases.

- **Stand-in service keys.** Programs on a teammate's computer and in a workspace-limited desk shell receive a stand-in value; the real key is added on the way to the service (GitHub, OpenAI, and Anthropic built in).
- **Separate sign-in browser.** Logins, sign-in codes, passkeys, and sessions stay in a separate sign-in browser on the teammate's computer and are typed there.
- **Only the newer check counts.** A computer has to prove itself to the desk with the check that came in computer 0.13. One that answers with the older, weaker check, drops back from the newer one, or isn't the computer the desk made gets no secrets. A computer from before 0.13 gets what it got in 0.49.
- **Kept across restarts.** Restarting Hotline while a teammate's computer runs keeps its secrets setup: the desk keeps the computer's key in the system's secret store and deletes it when the computer is removed. When the desk can't check a computer, keys waiting for it say "restart the computer".

### Other changes

- **Open loops.** Unfinished items carry across chapters in the teammate's pane with Ask and Dismiss, close when their scheduled job ends, and go quiet after two weeks.
- **Drawn faces.** Teammates can draw their own faces, which blink while they work.
- **Team list order.** Drag teammates in the team list to reorder them.
- **Document viewer.** CSV files render as tables, Office files and HTML open outside Hotline, and unsupported files offer a download instead of raw text.
- **Model speed.** Models with a fast tier or multiple speeds show a speed choice beside effort.
- **Clearer instructions for teammates.** Teammates are told which card fits each ask (signing in, a login, a yes or no, or something only you can do), never to ask for a password or code in chat or by voice, when a message needs your approval, when to stop and wait, and what to do at a usage limit.
- **Briefings sent once.** A Claude or Codex teammate reopened after a restart is no longer given its standing instructions twice. After its conversation is compacted, it gets them again.

### Fixes

- Speech provider errors on calls (out of credit, bad key, rate limit) report once and keep the answer on screen instead of saying "Sorry, say that again".
- Spoken call approvals read a short plain summary instead of full commands, paths, and IDs.
- Call prompts no longer leak the desk's turn count into teammate replies.
- Call panes show "Call" once.
- Calls resume listening as soon as a teammate stops speaking, and words said while an earlier turn is closing are answered instead of dropped.
- Replies no longer render multiple times at the bottom of a conversation.
- Failed turns in call, job, and helper panes show a plain sentence instead of raw error data.
- Follow-up quotes no longer reappear after switching teammates.
- Local speech models are pinned to fixed versions so upstream changes do not break installation.
- A teammate's message with a field it doesn't take is refused and names the right ones, so a hand-off is never sent as a question by mistake.
- A resumed Codex teammate no longer shows as working, or holds back its inbox, for five minutes after a restart.
- Words said over a reply interrupt it however many replies came before.
- Calls never say a placeholder such as "(Empty reply)", "Nothing." or "N/A".
- On a Mac, a headset that was playing stereo when a call began no longer leaves the call hearing silence while the meter moves, and the teammate's reply no longer moves the microphone meter.
- A Mac with no microphone says "No microphone found" when a call starts instead of failing, and a microphone unplugged mid-call ends the call saying so.

### Security

This release follows a review of the whole app. Fixes that change behavior:

- Skills are written through checked folder handles, so a link planted in a workspace cannot redirect the write.
- A phone or other paired device can no longer answer desk-only cards, and a typed answer is marked as typed.
- Passkey requests have a deadline and are bound to the requesting site, both kept by the desk.
- Shared sign-in cookies are read from the browser over a pipe instead of a local debugging port. On Windows, cookies from Chromium browsers are not offered for now.
- A teammate's workspace folder is tied to the folder it was granted, so swapping the folder for a link elsewhere no longer widens its reach.
- On Linux with kernel 6.12 or newer, a workspace-limited teammate's shell can no longer connect to the desktop session's abstract sockets. Older kernels cannot enforce this, and the shell's own description says so.
- A computer gets a new access token every time it is created.
- The computer's gate forwards only its own tool path, a desk token is no longer passed on a command line, and desk requests no longer follow redirects.

### Known limits

- Teammate messages cannot yet cross to a linked desk. 0.49 could pass a message to another desk, and 0.50 does not; a teammate that tries is told so.
- Non-final loop runs do not wait for a reply; one arriving after the next run starts goes quietly to main chat.
- On Windows, cookies can't be shared from Chrome, Brave, or Edge yet; only Firefox is offered. Sign in on the teammate's browser instead.
- 0.49 was the last version for Intel Macs. On a Mac, Hotline requires Apple silicon.
- The phone app does not have the new inbox rows, approval and limit cards, or new secret labels yet. A phone update follows; the phone loses nothing it has today.
- Secrets on a teammate's computer: reviews of this work found ways a program there could still get at a login, or use a key to make a new credential. The desk refuses the known key-making routes for GitHub, OpenAI, and Anthropic, and more fixes come with computer 0.13; the rest is being worked on. Treat a secret granted to a teammate as something that teammate's programs may be able to reach. `gh` in the Mac desk shell does not work with a stand-in key (curl, git, Python, and Node do). Tested on Apple's container runtime and Podman, not on Docker.
- The built-in voice is English only and is not offered on ARM Linux. It has run on the Mac; Windows and Intel Linux passed the build but had not been heard when these notes were written.
- Call acknowledgments and progress updates are on by default; acknowledgments need a call assistant model from a connected provider. Switch them off in Settings › Voice.
- Local speech models download uncompressed from fixed versions, so downloads are larger (Whisper is about 1 GB).
