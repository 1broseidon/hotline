A · One list, filtered
  • Everything is one row type: kind mark, title, one line of state or latest step, time.
  • Opens on Active: Needs you, Live, Today. Earlier, routine runs and read notes fold into one row that expands in place. Counts only on Needs you and Live.
  • A kind chip shows all of that kind, live and ended. Search covers everything. The last filter is kept per teammate.
  • Notes (asides) are rows too, linked to the thread they came from. The Notes chip shows only them.
  • Good for: one place, one mental model. Risk: a long day makes a long list, so search and chips carry it.
B · Live on top, history below
  • Live work (and anything waiting on you) gets cards with a 3-line ticker and Stop / Open.
  • Everything ended goes into a dense one-line history with search, a kind menu, and Threads | Notes.
  • Notes hang off their thread as a count, and open under it; the Notes view is a plain feed.
  • Good for: watching work. Risk: two layouts in one pane; ended things are one tap further.
