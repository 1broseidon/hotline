## Hotline 0.43.0: your accounts, one picker, and keys you approve

- **Several accounts of any provider.** Add a provider you already have and name each account ("Anthropic · Work", "Anthropic · Personal"). Each teammate runs on its own account, and a removed account never falls back to another.
- **One picker.** Model, account, effort and agent in one menu: the model in use at the top with an effort slider, your accounts, then the agents. Pick a model under Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Google Antigravity or Grok Build and the teammate moves there, finishing its turn first.
- **No waiting on agents.** The desk reads its agents' model lists as it starts, and an agent that needs signing in says so on its row.
- **Credentials that go only to the vault.** A teammate asks for a login or passkey on a card; what you type goes straight to the vault. Any login or passkey can be Ask me, approved each time or for 10 minutes, from the desk or the phone.
- **Your browser's session, when you say so.** When a teammate opens a site you're signed in to, the desk offers your existing cookie, always asking, and takes it back on Remove.
- **Computer 0.11.** New computers get the extension as a card on the page, Ask me keys and cookie offers.
- **A calmer window.** Settings in the rail's dock, edge-to-edge panes, and the teammate's face as its menu in the top row.
- **Faster on long conversations.** Cached history, a roster kept in memory and leaner streaming.
