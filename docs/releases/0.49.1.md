## Hotline 0.49.1: the line under a reply reads as one, and one notification per ask

- **The line under a reply reads from the left.** What a teammate left behind, the sites it read and how long it took now run together under the reply, like `✓ Comparison chart of 16 agent tools · 6m 55s ›`, instead of the time sitting at the far right. When there is only the time, it sits at the left, under the reply it belongs to.
- **One phone notification per ask.** When a teammate starts helpers and waits on them, your phone gets nothing while it waits and one notification when it's done, with the last thing it said. On a call you still hear what it says as it says it, and anything that needs you, like a permission, still reaches your phone at once.
