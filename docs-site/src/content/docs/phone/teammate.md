Tap the teammate's name at the head of its conversation to open its
**Settings**. The desk's owner also finds there the pages that say what the
teammate signs in with and what it may reach. They show the same as the
teammate's pane on the desk: see [Sign-ins](/docs/setup/sign-ins/) and
[Desk browser](/docs/setup/browser/).

## Secrets

<!-- TODO(screenshot, iOS simulator): a teammate's Secrets page. -->

**Browser and secrets**, in the teammate's **Settings** on the owner's
phone, lists the logins and passkeys the teammate is given under
**Secrets**. They belong to the teammate, not to its computer, so they show
and hold whether or not it has one.

## Cookies

<!-- TODO(screenshot, iOS simulator): a teammate's Cookies page, one shared site open: Keep fresh and Remove. -->

Each site whose cookies you shared with the teammate is a row, saying which
browser it came from. Open one for **Keep fresh** and **Remove**.

## Permissions

<!-- TODO(screenshot, iOS simulator): a teammate's Permissions page with Local addresses open. -->

What the teammate may reach, with **Local addresses**: the addresses on
your computer or network you told its browser to always allow, each with
**Remove**. A removed address asks again next time.
