Toad is a room for a team of agents, on your machine. Each teammate has a name, a goal, its own tools and, when it needs one, its own computer. They ask each other for help, work on a schedule, and everything they do is on the record in one window. Open source under MIT or Apache-2.0, self-hosted, no account.
