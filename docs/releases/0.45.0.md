## Hotline 0.45.0: every agent at its best, and the work at a glance

- **Choose before you send.** Claude Code, Codex, Grok Build, Cursor and Antigravity teammates offer their models, effort and modes whether they are running or not, and a choice made at rest is the one the next session starts on. Your choice always wins over what an agent reports.
- **One pill, one menu.** The model, its effort and the teammate's face are one pill; its menu says who the teammate is, what it runs on, effort as one row of tabs, the agent's newest five models and Change agent. A model switch lands on the effort you'd expect and remembers it.
- **Your plan, without asking.** A small gauge beside the teammate shows how much of the week and the five hours your Claude Code, Codex or Grok Build plan has used, read the way each agent's own tools read it, with the runway: when you'd run out at this pace.
- **Fast mode.** On the models that offer it, a Fast toggle under the effort tabs, with what it costs beside it.
- **Cursor and Antigravity, properly.** Cursor's base models with reasoning, context size, fast mode and Run Everything; Antigravity's thinking levels and modes. Grok Build lists Grok 4.7.
- **The work at a glance.** A turn ends with one caption under its last bubble: the sites it read, its steps, files and any failure. It opens the side pane, which keeps the turn's shape and sources in view while its steps scroll, and steps between turns.
- **Side by side.** Open a teammate's chat in a window of its own from the rail's menu. Markdown files open rendered.
- **Windows, Linux and light mode.** The window's controls have a row of their own on Windows and Linux, the pane docks beside the conversation after a fresh install, and light mode meets AA contrast throughout.
- **Fixes.** A computer that cannot start says why, the computer key goes when it stops, and a colleague's thread stops showing Thinking when its turn ends.
