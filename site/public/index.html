Four things my team did this morning.

  • Ran a morning report on its own schedule.

    Timo’s Devils stat tracker ran at 9:04 and wrote up last night’s game, player by player. The next run is already on the calendar.

  • Drew its own avatar.

    Asked for a shinier picture, Goldie generated one, set it, and backed the old one up first. The face in every window here is hers.

  • Got a computer of its own with one switch.

    Its own folder, its own permissions and, when you flip it, its own desktop.

  • Took a call, then wrote it up.

    We talked through going fully local, out loud. When the call parked, her report was in the thread.

A browser for each, or a whole desktop.

Every teammate comes with a browser for quick work: read a page, check a fix, sign in where you allow it. For heavier jobs, one switch gives it a Linux desktop instead. Either way, you can watch or take the mouse.

My team stays home. You don’t have to.

My teammates run on your computer. Pair your iPhone and they come with you: answer them, approve what they ask from the notification, call them, take over a teammate’s computer. At home it reaches your desk over Wi-Fi; anywhere else, through your own VPN or a Hotline server, encrypted end to end.
