title: Hotline on your phone
description: Your teammates in your pocket, talking to a desk on your Mac or a server.
---

Hotline for iPhone is a window onto a desk: the desktop app on your Mac, or
`hotline serve` on a server. The teammates run on the desk. The phone shows
their conversations as they happen, lets you answer them, and lets you watch
and drive a teammate's computer.

:::note[Beta]
Hotline for iPhone is in beta on TestFlight and not yet on the App Store.
[Join the beta](TESTFLIGHT_PUBLIC_LINK), then install it from TestFlight.
:::

## What the phone can do

- Talk to every teammate, send photos and files, and reply to a message.
- Answer what a teammate is waiting on: a permission, a **Needs you** card,
  or a passkey. It works from the notification too.
- Add a teammate, rename it, change its goal, or remove it.
- Watch a teammate's computer and take control of it.
- Link several desks, and switch between them or see them all at once.

## What stays at the desk

What a teammate is allowed to reach stays at the desk: its folders, tools,
MCP servers and computer. So do providers and keys. The phone can't widen a
teammate, and neither can anyone holding it.

Start with [Link a desk](/docs/phone/pair/).
