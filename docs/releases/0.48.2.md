## Hotline 0.48.2: a browser for every teammate on a server

- **A browser on your server.** Teammates without a computer on a `hotline serve` desk now get a web browser too, and your phone is its screen: open it from the globe in the chat header, Take control, tap and type, and Give it back. It's opt-in: install or update with `--browser` to add WebKitGTK and Xvfb (about 500 MB), on x86_64 and ARM. Without them the server runs as before.
- **Drive it from a linked desktop.** A desktop linked to the server opens that teammate's browser in a viewer of its own, from the corner picture or a sign-in card's Open Browser, with the same controls.
- **Visit anyway, from away.** Past a certificate the browser refused, take control from a linked desktop and choose Visit anyway. The phone's button comes in its next update.
- **No cookie sharing on a server.** A server has no browsers of yours to read, so its sign-in card offers Sign In and Open Browser.
