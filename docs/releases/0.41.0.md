## Hotline 0.41.0: a team lead, jobs with threads of their own, and one way to the chat

- **A team lead.** One teammate leads the rest, marked with a star. Your first teammate leads; right-click any teammate to make it lead, and with two or more and none chosen, Hotline asks you to pick.
- **Jobs run in their own threads.** Every scheduled job and loop gets a thread in the new Jobs group, with its next run and its last post. Firings never land in your chat: a job posts only what's worth saying, remembers where it stood, skips a run that would overlap and suggests a better interval, and can be talked to, stopped mid-run or cancelled from its row.
- **Everything reaches the chat the same way.** Work threads, jobs and teammates on any agent post to the chat with a stamp saying where it came from, and files sent from a thread land in the chat too.
- **Subagents and jobs are the teammate itself, with one focus.** They have the same model, permissions and computer, and can search the conversation they came from.
- **Under the hood.** The core is split into focused crates (contract, store, tools, room, MCP client, voice engines) for cleaner boundaries and faster, more targeted tests.
