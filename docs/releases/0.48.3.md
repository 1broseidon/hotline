## Hotline 0.48.3: a server's browser as fast as its computer, and your messages stay in view

- **A server's browser, live.** A desktop linked to a server now watches and drives a teammate's browser the way it does a computer: only what changes on the page is sent, and your pointer, wheel and keys reach it at once, hover and drag included. A click shows in about 30 ms instead of a third of a second, scrolling runs at about 50 frames a second, and an idle page sends nothing. The phone gets the live view in its next release.
- **Your messages stay in view.** A message you send no longer lands behind the composer until the teammate answers.
- **Scrolling up keeps your place.** Older lines loading in no longer throw the view down the conversation.
