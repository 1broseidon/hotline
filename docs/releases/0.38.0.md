## Hotline 0.38.0: live voice calls

- **Calls answer as fast as a person.** A call to the desk or a teammate runs on a live voice model: replies start as you stop talking, and talking over it stops it. Your teammate still does the work. The live voice passes your words on as you said them and says only what the teammate's turn gave it. Pick the model in Settings › Live calls: Grok Voice on your Grok subscription or an xAI key, GPT-Live on your ChatGPT subscription, or GPT Realtime on an OpenAI key. Legacy voice, the old transcribe-and-speak path, is still there, and a call falls back to it when no live model can open.
- **A call to a teammate sounds like the teammate.** On a direct call it speaks for itself ("one sec, I'll pull that up"), acknowledges a request once, and keeps you posted without repeating itself. A teammate's voice setting lists the voices of whichever live model calls use.
- **Nothing said on a call is lost.** The call card's transcript key shows both sides. What you said on the call is tagged in the conversation, and a question the call ended on is answered in writing afterwards. Calls one after another fold into one line.
- **The call key is in the composer.** A soundwave key beside the mic places the call, and the call opens as a card on the conversation with the teammate's face and its voice moving beneath. The desk's call key and a real search field sit in a dock at the foot of the team rail. The title bar holds New teammate and Settings, and the old … menu's Shortcuts and About live in Settings now.
- **A few words, and the rest a press away.** Tooltips are a few words long, and a call that can't start says why in two or three words, such as No microphone or Sign-in needed, with the full reason behind an info key.
