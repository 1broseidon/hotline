## Hotline 0.35.1: a calmer Settings, and a second chance when a turn fails

- **Settings, redesigned.** Every screen now works like the setup wizard: each setting shows what it's set to, and opens only when you want to change it.
- **Try again.** If a reply fails because of the network or the provider, tap Try again, on the desktop or your phone. Your message isn't sent twice.
- **Know when there's an update.** A small card tells you when a new version is ready, and the notes read properly.
- **Reach your desk through your own tunnel.** Paste a Cloudflare Tunnel or ngrok address in Settings › Remote, and your phone uses it away from home.
- **New shortcuts.** Call your agent or Call the desk from any app.
