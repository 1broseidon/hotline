Two families
  • In it (DM, work thread, call): a conversation. Work folds behind captions because you're there.
  • Watching (subagent, handoff, pair, command, job): the work shows live without a click.
  • Every pane has a kind label and a family line, so nothing reads as a plain chat.
Per type
  • Handoff: like a subagent, but the composer stays, because you can step in.
  • Pair: two-sided, both faces, Stop exchange.
  • Command: the command, live output, Stop.
  • Call: lines labelled by speaker, delegated work folded inline.
  • Job: schedule and next run, then each run as its own section.
Fixes regardless
  • In your chat, a pair's line says "Thread". It should say "Pair".
  • Jobs have no line of their own in the chat.
  • Background commands and subagents share a kind. Split the label: "Command" vs "Subagent".
