## Hotline 0.36.1: the right name on a handoff, and cookies on a Mac

- **Handoffs name who's doing the work.** Opened from the teammate who handed it over, a handoff used to say that teammate handed it to themselves. It now names the teammate doing the work, on the bar, on the reply and in the list.
- **Cookies on a Mac say what's in the way.** When macOS keeps Hotline out of a browser's data, the cookie picker names the browser and takes you to Full Disk Access instead of saying no browser was found.
- **More browsers found on a Mac.** Firefox, Chrome Beta, Dev and Canary, Chromium, the Edge previews, Arc and Vivaldi, including apps in your own Applications folder.
