## Hotline 0.39.0: the voice on a call is the teammate

- **A call can do the looking up itself.** On a call to a teammate, the live voice answers conversation and looks things up with the teammate's own tools (its conversation, schedules, the web and its granted servers) without waiting on a turn. Only real work goes to the teammate's working session. It works on Grok Voice, GPT Realtime and GPT-Live.
- **The chat stays clean.** What's said on a call stays on the call. The conversation gets one summary in the teammate's own words when you hang up, and every 5 minutes on a long call, plus anything you ask it to put in the chat. Small talk and sound checks never land there.
- **A server can keep running.** When a teammate starts a server or a watcher and replies, its turn is over: the call goes back to listening and the server runs on as a chip beside the subagents, with its output and a Stop key. If it stops on its own, the teammate tells you.
- **Off is an option.** Settings › Providers can turn Live calls and Images off. About and Shortcuts now sit in a quiet footer under the Settings sections.
- **GPT-Live tool requests work.** Asking GPT-Live to look something up no longer leaves the call silent.
